Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common hobbyist and collector queries

We understand that mint box condition is vital for collectors. Every figurine and diecast model car is wrapped in bubble film and shipped in heavy-duty double-corrugated outer boxes reinforced with edge protectors to prevent crush damage during transit.
All UK mainland orders over £50.00 qualify for FREE standard tracked delivery (typically 2-3 business days). For orders under £50, standard delivery is a flat rate of £4.99. Orders placed before 2:00 PM GMT are dispatched the same day.
The Collector's Vault lets you track your hobby inventory in your browser using localStorage. You can categorize any saved product as "Interested", "Owned", or "Not Interested", filter them via tabs, and move items straight to your cart whenever you are ready to buy.
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must remain unopened in their original manufacturer packaging with security seals intact. If an item arrives with manufacturing defects or transit damage, we provide an immediate prepaid return label and replacement.
